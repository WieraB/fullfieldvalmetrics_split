Definitions
--------------------------------------------------------------
  Positive change = split value is higher than pooled value.
  Error/interval metrics: lower is better; negative change means split improves.
  Coverage: better means closer to 0.95; coverage error is |coverage - 0.95|.
  Coverage-error changes are absolute changes in that distance.
  Accuracy: higher is better; all studies use a 10 % relative tolerance.
  Accuracy changes are absolute accuracy points (0-1).
  Win rates are over common model configurations.
  independent-sample test.
